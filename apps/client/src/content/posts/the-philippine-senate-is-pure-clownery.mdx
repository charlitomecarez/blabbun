Yeah… watching TV and scrolling through social media lately has genuinely been giving me a headache.

Every single day I open my phone and it’s just another episode of “How Much More Embarrassing Can the Philippine Government Get?” and somehow they ALWAYS find a way to top the previous season finale 😭

Like bro… the inefficiency is actually insane.

There’s drama everywhere.
Every department got beef.
Every politician acting like they in a reality TV show instead of running a country.

And the thing that pushed me over the edge recently?

…and was STILL GETTING PAID.

EXCUSE ME???

How is that even real?

Imagine telling your boss:
“Yeah my bad gang, I vanished for half a year.”

And then you STILL got money hitting your bank account like nothing happened 💀

That’s the type of thing that makes normal people mad because it’s not just unfair — it’s disrespectful.

You got people working overtime, barely surviving inflation, showing up every single day trying to make ends meet…

Meanwhile this dude basically rage quit existence and still got rewarded for it.

And the craziest part?
Everybody online was saying the exact same thing.

Every comment section was just people collectively losing their minds asking:
“WHERE IS THIS MAN???”

Fast forward to now…

HE FINALLY RETURNS.


Not to work.
Not to explain himself.
Not to apologize.

Nah.

And it somehow got EVEN MORE ridiculous when this man finally showed up at the senate office 😭

Because instead of walking in quietly, explaining himself, and acting like a normal functioning adult…

this dude came in already making drama.

Bro was ranting, throwing energy around, acting irritated that people were questioning him like HE wasn’t the one who disappeared for half a year 💀

And then — because apparently the universe itself wanted to contribute to the comedy — this man TRIPS ON THE STAIRS 😭

BRO WHAT 😭

That’s straight-up alarming.
